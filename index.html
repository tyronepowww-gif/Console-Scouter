Your privacy and this fictional reading

This is a fictional, Dragon Ball-inspired scouter experience. It does not measure heart rate, HRV, stress, health, fitness, medical status, or real power level. Do not use it for medical, safety, exercise, or training decisions.

The camera opens only after you choose to begin a scan and approve your browser's permission prompt. In this local-only version, temporary camera frames are processed in your browser to generate a game-like reading. Camera video, images, and audio are not uploaded, transmitted, sold, or shared.

Fictional scan history is optional and, if enabled, stays only in this browser on this device until you clear it.
